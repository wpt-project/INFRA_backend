Help and feedback
Sealine help, feedback, Terms of Service, and Privacy Policy.
Draft document: Terms of Service & Privacy Policy v1.1 is marked as a draft prepared for legal review and should not be treated as an approved production policy.
SecuritySealine is described as an end-to-end encrypted messaging service. The policy states that encryption keys are stored on the user's device, and for Web, in the browser client.
New-device loginLogging in on a new device automatically signs out another active phone or Web session tied to the account.
Data collectionThe draft lists phone number, profile information, optional contact matching, device/technical data, and message metadata. Normal message content is not collected in readable form.
Web clientThe draft says the Web client stores only data needed for service function, including session/link state, and keeps Web encryption keys locally in the browser.
Your rightsThe draft describes access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection/restriction, and applicable non-discrimination rights, subject to jurisdiction.
ContactThe draft currently contains a placeholder for legal/support and privacy contact details. Final contact details must be inserted after establishment.
